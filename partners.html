10 Year Anniversary in 2026

Celebrating a Decade of Innovation

In 2026, ThermalSkinIQ Inc. proudly celebrates the 10th anniversary of ThermalSkinIQ, a decade marked by groundbreaking advancements in clean energy heating solutions. From pioneering the world’s first UL 2683-certified carbon veil radiant in-floor heating technology to achieving a 75% reduction in energy consumption compared to traditional baseboard systems, ThermalSkinIQ has transformed residential and commercial heating.

With successful market entry in the U.S., strategic partnerships with industry leaders like AvCarb, MP Global, OJ Controls, and a commitment to sustainability through AEThermal Solutions, we’ve laid a strong foundation for global impact. Here’s to a decade of innovation and a future of driving energy efficiency and environmental stewardship worldwide!

10 Years

AvCarb Material Solutions

Advanced Carbon Engineering

AvCarb Material Solutions is a premier global manufacturer of highly engineered carbon-based materials. By partnering with AvCarb, ThermalSkinIQ integrates cutting-edge engineered prepreg carbon woven fabric technologies directly into our thermal systems.

Their advanced carbon fabrics ensure exceptional thermal conductivity, unmatched physical durability, and consistent heat distribution, allowing us to deliver the most reliable and efficient discrete heating solutions on the market.

OJ Electronics

Strategic Control & HVAC Partner

We are excited to collaborate with OJ Electronics as a key strategic partner in our global expansion. For over 60 years, OJ Electronics has been a leader in designing innovative electronic controls for electric underfloor heating, HVAC systems, and drives.

With a presence in over 40 countries, OJ Electronics provides expanded expertise, resources, and a shared commitment to sustainability.

This powerful partnership combines cutting-edge control technology with world-class engineering, enabling us to deliver more advanced, reliable, and eco-conscious solutions to our customers around the globe.

MP Global Products

Strategic North American Distribution & Mfg.

  • Founded: 1997 | Headquarters: Norfolk, Nebraska, USA
  • Expertise: High-performance, sustainable building-material solutions, including acoustical underlayments, thermal packaging, and floor heating systems.
  • Sustainability: Up-cycles pre- and post-consumer fiber waste through patented thermal-bonding processes, diverting hundreds of tons of material from landfills.
  • Flagship Products: QuietWalk® acoustical underlayments and QuietBoard® support systems (10M+ sq. ft. monthly capacity).

Strategic Value to ThermalSkinIQ

Provides an established, scalable, and eco-aligned manufacturing platform. MP Global’s products have been tested with ThermalSkinIQ, demonstrating strong compatibility and supporting rapid manufacturing growth.

A distribution agreement has been signed providing a path to market through MP Global's established wholesale & retail channels. Discussions are currently in place for an MOU which would make MP Global our first domestic manufacturing facility located in the North Eastern United States.

AEThermal Solutions

In-House Laboratory & R&D Hub

ThermalSkinIQ Inc. has collaborated with leading manufacturing and industry experts to establish AEThermal Solutions.

We utilized our In-House Laboratory to reinvent a prototype design that LaminaHeat had been struggling with developing. We leveraged our Graphite/Carbon team's years of design engineering and scaled manufacturing processes to completely re-design the world's first Engineered Prepreg Carbon Woven Fabric Primary heat technology and achieve the first UL2683 certification for All Cementous and Non-Cementous in-floor heating Applications.

United Brotherhood of Carpenters & The NCC

North American Training Partnership

The United Brotherhood of Carpenters Union (U.S.) and the National Construction Council (Canada) have partnered with us to integrate ThermalSkinIQ Discrete into their North American training program. As the largest trade union in North America, they operate over 250 training facilities and invest over $200 million per year, training over 500,000 union members.

Benefits of Integration:

  • Members will be certified to perform installations, providing trades with more jobs.
  • Provides ThermalSkinIQ with the necessary training services for Flooring Installers & Electricians in both countries.
  • All installations completed by trained certified member trades are fully warrantied by the union.

Industry Shift: The HVAC industry is changing, with Natural Gas heat no longer being allowed in new builds or renovations in some cities and municipalities. As this transition takes place, trades in the HVAC market segment are well-trained for easy adoption of the ThermalSkinIQ Discrete heating system.
