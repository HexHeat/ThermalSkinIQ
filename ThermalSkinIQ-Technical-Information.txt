ThermalSkinIQ™
Intelligent radiant surfaces
Powered by InfraWeave™ carbon-fabric technology

TECHNOLOGY OVERVIEW
ThermalSkinIQ transforms approved building surfaces into low-profile radiant-heating systems. InfraWeave is a highly carbonized, orthogonally interlaced conductive fabric arranged in an engineered serpentine configuration.

TECHNOLOGY DESCRIPTION
The heating assembly incorporates a woven conductive-fabric resistance element arranged in a controlled serpentine configuration within a panel assembly. The platform is designed for broad-area radiant heating and integration into approved floor, wall, or ceiling assemblies.

ELECTRICAL DESIGN
Assemblies may be engineered for specified 120 V or 240 V circuits. Resistance, wattage, current, terminals, temperature controls, sensors, safety devices, and compatible finishes must be coordinated with the approved electrical and thermal design, installation requirements, and applicable codes and certifications.

BASIS OF DESIGN
ThermalSkinIQ radiant-heating panels incorporating InfraWeave highly carbonized, orthogonally interlaced conductive-fabric heating elements arranged in an engineered serpentine configuration and designed for operation on the specified 120 V or 240 V electrical circuit.

APPLICATION
The system is intended for approved commercial and residential floor, wall, or ceiling assemblies. Application is subject to project-specific thermal and electrical design, installation requirements, finished-surface limitations, and applicable certifications.
