Preferred Merchant Vendors Onboard Training

Program 1 - Merchant/Vendor Clinics

Welcome to the comprehensive training program designed as a progressive module based rollout for commercial/wholesalers supplying trade contractors. This program is designed to equip you with all the knowledge and skills you need to excel in providing customer support. Our courses cover a wide range of topics, starting with basic customer service requirements, basic application and installations. Then as client progresses further in module training to “Preferred Vendor Status”. Which covers comprehensive applications and installation process requirements, ensuring you have everything you need to provide exceptional service to your clients on larger commercial projects.

Introduction to Product & Basic Installation

  • “Engineered Prepreg Carbon Woven Fabric” heater technology.
  • How our technology differs from Resistive Cable, Baseboards, and Hydronic in-floor heating (Delta T Δ measurements).
  • What is Prepreg design?
  • Review of documentation and training videos.
