ThermalSkinIQ™

Intelligent radiant surfaces

Powered by InfraWeave™ carbon woven-fabric technology

ThermalSkinIQ transforms approved building surfaces into low-profile radiant-heating systems. At the core is InfraWeave, a highly carbonized, orthogonally interlaced conductive fabric arranged in an engineered serpentine configuration for residential and commercial floor, wall and ceiling applications.

The technology

The difference is in the weave

InfraWeave uses a highly carbonized woven conductive fabric to create broad-area radiant heating. The interlaced element is arranged in an engineered serpentine path across the panel assembly.

Woven construction

An orthogonally interlaced fabric architecture designed to combine electrical conductivity, flexibility and broad-area heat generation.

Serpentine configuration

A continuous heating element arranged in a controlled path to meet the electrical and thermal needs of the panel assembly.

Surface distribution

The panel architecture is designed to distribute heat across its surface rather than concentrate output along a single conventional heating wire.

Low-profile integration

A flexible heating architecture designed for approved assemblies where installation depth and usable space matter.

Configurable design

Panel dimensions, element length, serpentine spacing, watt density and thermal-spreader construction can be configured for different assemblies.

Electrical options

Systems can be engineered for specified 120 V or 240 V circuits, subject to application-specific design and applicable electrical codes.

Independent zones

The platform is intended to support room-, zone- or surface-level heating management and temperature sensing.

Residential and commercial

The technology is a Primary heating system, provides Far-Infrared heat, designed for homes, multi-unit developments and commercial interiors.

Technical information

Open a specification section for basis-of-design wording and system requirements. Final specifications must be coordinated with the approved product, assembly and project design.

Basis of design

ThermalSkinIQ radiant-heating panels incorporating InfraWeave highly carbonized, orthogonally interlaced conductive-fabric heating elements arranged in an engineered serpentine configuration and designed for operation on the specified 120 V or 240 V electrical circuit.

Short specification description

Low-profile electric radiant-heating panel incorporating a highly carbonized woven conductive-fabric resistance element configured in a serpentine path.

Extended specification considerations

Provide a ThermalSkinIQ electric radiant-heating system incorporating InfraWeave highly carbonized woven conductive-fabric heating elements. Arrange each element in a controlled serpentine configuration within the panel assembly. Coordinate system voltage, resistance, wattage, current, electrical terminals, temperature controls, sensors, safety devices and finished-surface compatibility with the approved electrical and thermal design.

Application and performance design

The system is intended for approved commercial and residential floor, wall or ceiling assemblies, subject to project-specific thermal and electrical design, installation requirements, finished-surface limitations and applicable certifications. Heating-element geometry, pass spacing, thermal spreader, controls and panel output should be coordinated with the approved assembly and project heat-loss calculations.

Performance claims should be limited to what is supported: designed or engineered characteristics, modeled estimates, or validated results from representative physical assemblies. Compatible finishes must be confirmed through testing and installation requirements; actual performance depends on the complete installed assembly.
