Powered by InfraWeave™ carbon-fabric technology

Intelligent radiant surfaces

ThermalSkinIQ™ transforms approved building surfaces into low-profile radiant-heating systems. At the core of the platform is InfraWeave™, a highly carbonized, orthogonally interlaced conductive fabric arranged in an engineered serpentine configuration.

The technology

The difference is in the weave

InfraWeave™ uses highly carbonized woven conductive fabric to produce broad-area radiant heat. Its orthogonally interlaced architecture is formed into a controlled serpentine path designed to distribute electrical energy across the heating surface.

Rather than relying on a conventional cable or isolated resistance wire, the heating function is carried through a woven conductive element arranged across the panel surface.

A platform built in layers

  1. 1

    Woven heating element

    Highly carbonized, orthogonally interlaced conductive fabric.

  2. 2

    Panel assembly

    A serpentine element integrated into configurable, low-profile panels.

  3. 3

    Sensing and control

    A platform designed to support temperature sensing and independently managed zones.

Designed for integration

One technology platform. Flexible configurations.

ThermalSkinIQ is designed for residential and commercial projects, including new construction, renovation and retrofit, subject to project-specific design and approved assembly requirements.

Broad-area radiant heating

The woven conductive element is arranged across a panel surface to provide a broad-area heating architecture.

Configurable panel design

Panel dimensions, element layout and thermal-spreader construction can be configured for different building assemblies.

Zones and controls

The platform is intended to support independently managed heating areas, temperature sensing and future building-control integration.

Electrical design

Heating assemblies can be engineered for specified 120 V or 240 V circuits. Resistance, wattage, current, terminals, controls and safety devices must be coordinated with the approved electrical design, installation requirements and applicable codes.

Engineered for approved building assemblies

ThermalSkinIQ is intended for integration into approved floor, wall or ceiling assemblies. Final application depends on project-specific thermal and electrical design, installation requirements, finished-surface limitations and applicable certifications.
