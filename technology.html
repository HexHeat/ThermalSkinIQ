ThermalSkinIQ™

An Intelligent Far-Infrared Radiant PRIMARY Heating System for Residential & Commercial Applications

Powered by InfraWeave™ carbon woven-fabric technology

ThermalSkinIQ transforms approved building surfaces into low-profile far-infrared radiant-heating systems. At the core is InfraWeave, a highly carbonized superconductor, orthogonally interlaced conductive fabric arranged in an engineered serpentine configuration for residential and commercial floor, wall and ceiling applications.

The technology

How ThermalSkinIQ Works with your Building

ThermalSkinIQ Heater is a pre-wired engineered prepreg carbon woven fabric, far-infrared radiant underfloor heating system for use in residential and commercial applications. The line includes 120 V and 240 V heaters in multiple widths and lengths, allowing designers, distributors, installers, and project owners to select a configuration that fits the open heated area and electrical plan.

Warmth from a broad surface

The weave spreads heat through the full-surface panel, producing far-infrared thermal radiation. The floors, walls, and ceiling become the heat source, rather than warmth coming from a single spot in the room.

Building materials hold warmth

Materials such as drywall, tile and concrete absorb heat, building up thermal mass that is released gradually, even after the system switches off. How much warmth they store depends on its density.

The building envelope limits heat loss

The building envelope is the shell that separates indoors from outdoors. Interior materials can store heat and release it gradually. Insulation and air sealing help limit heat loss. ThermalSkinIQ supplies the heat; it does not replace insulation or air sealing.

Planned room by room

Heater coverage is planned for individual rooms or larger zones, each controlled by its own thermostat. Unoccupied rooms can be turned down when not in use to further conserve energy consumption.

Designed for integration

A platform for different buildings and projects

ThermalSkinIQ is UL Certifed for installation under Tile/Stone & Resilient floor types (LVT, SPC, Laminate & Vinyl) in residential and commercial construction.

Floor, wall and ceiling

Each heater is a low-profile far-infrared super conductor engineered for approved floor systems. Once installed, a system creates homeostasis within the building envelope with no stratification, meaning the temperature at floor level is virtually the same as at the ceiling.

New construction

Configurable heater architecture engineered for seamless installation using existing building methods, including installation of the heaters, electrical, architectural, and interior-finish systems.

Designed into the building.

Renovation and retrofit

Its low-profile design makes installation simple, and at only 3 mm thick it minimizes the spatial impact of adding radiant heat to an existing building.

Advanced comfort without bulky equipment.

Commercial and multi-unit

Scalable technology for projects requiring repeatable panel configurations, independent zones, and coordinated control, consuming approximately 65% less energy than baseboard heaters.

Built to scale.

Cold-climate applications

A configurable electric radiant-heating platform for projects requiring responsive room- or zone-level heat.

Intelligent comfort, proven in demanding climates including climate zone 7.

Technical information

Open a specification section for basis-of-design wording and system requirements. Final specifications must be coordinated with the approved product, assembly and project design.

Basis of design

ThermalSkinIQ radiant-heating panels incorporating InfraWeave highly carbonized, orthogonally interlaced conductive-fabric heating elements arranged in an engineered serpentine configuration and designed for operation on the specified 120 V or 240 V electrical circuit.

Short specification description

Low-profile electric radiant-heating panel incorporating a highly carbonized woven conductive-fabric resistance element configured in a serpentine path.

Extended specification considerations

Provide a ThermalSkinIQ electric radiant-heating system incorporating InfraWeave highly carbonized woven conductive-fabric heating elements. Arrange each element in a controlled serpentine configuration within the panel assembly. Coordinate system voltage, resistance, wattage, current, electrical terminals, temperature controls, sensors, safety devices and finished-surface compatibility with the approved electrical and thermal design.

Application and performance design

The system is intended for approved commercial and residential floor, wall or ceiling assemblies, subject to project-specific thermal and electrical design, installation requirements, finished-surface limitations and applicable certifications. Heating-element geometry, pass spacing, thermal spreader, controls and panel output should be coordinated with the approved assembly and project heat-loss calculations.

Performance claims should be limited to what is supported: designed or engineered characteristics, modeled estimates, or validated results from representative physical assemblies. Compatible finishes must be confirmed through testing and installation requirements; actual performance depends on the complete installed assembly.
