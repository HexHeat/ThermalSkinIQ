Designed for LVP, SPC, Laminate, & Vinyl Floors

A Revolution for Resilient-Surface Flooring

HexHeat Discrete Heaters represent a groundbreaking category in residential and commercial heating. The world’s first UL 2683 certified, full-surface, carbon-composite based far-infrared system engineered specifically as primary whole-home heating.

Unlike legacy systems that primarily heat air, an inefficient medium prone to rapid heat loss and stratification, HexHeat transforms the building itself into an efficient thermal mass.

HexHeat is installed directly beneath floating floors without the need for thin-set, self-leveler, or complex infrastructure. Its ultra-thin profile (approx. 2.2 mm) ensures that transition heights are less than half of legacy technologies, eliminating the need for bulky reducers between rooms.

Advanced Engineering & Performance

  • Aerospace-Grade Materials: Utilizing a proprietary carbon composite conductor, HexHeat uses a combination of convection and uniform far-infrared thermal flux, acting as a thermal superconductor, delivering uniform heat across 100% of the active surface area.
  • Low-Temperature Operation: The system operates at low watt densities and heater temperatures (engineered to prevent flooring from exceeding 29°C) while maintaining high radiant output, resulting in energy consumption up to 60% lower than traditional electric baseboards.
  • Efficiency: Rather than circulating hot air, HexHeat warming objects, surfaces, and people directly by charging the building structure. By covering 50% - 65% of floor area, it creates stable room-level homeostasis.
  • Seamless Installation: The "Dry Install" pathway allows for rapid project turnover, typically requiring only one man-hour per 100 sq. ft.

Key Benefits

HexHeat offers a silent, low-energy platform that outperforms traditional systems in reliability and lifetime cost.

  • Zoned Primary Heating: Offers precise temperature control per zone with no greater than a 2-degree variation across the living space.
  • Zero Maintenance: With no ducts, pumps, fans, or mechanical points of failure, maintenance requirements are virtually non-existent.
  • Unmatched Safety: As the first system to achieve UL 2683 certification, it has been rigorously tested for electrical safety, including Hi-pot testing to 2500V.
  • Architectural Freedom: The ultra-thin profile eliminates the need for height transitions or reducers between different rooms.

Flooring Substrates

HexHeat provides a fast, clean installation pathway for various flooring types, typically requiring only about one man-hour per 100 sq. ft. The performance is intrinsically linked to the subfloor environment.

Installation on Plywood Substrate

The world’s first in-floor primary heating technology designed to install directly beneath floating floors without thin-set or complex infrastructure. For wood-frame residential and commercial structures, HexHeat integrates seamlessly into standard subfloor assemblies.

  • The Thermal Buffer: A high-density underlayment (min 6mm, max 8mm) is installed directly over the plywood. This provides "channeling" depth for cold leads and acts as a thermal break.
  • Structural Integrity: Utilizing high-compression underlayments (e.g., QuietBoard®) maintains the structural requirements of resilient flooring, preventing "bounce".
  • Direct Placement: Mats are laid directly atop the underlayment. A "dry install" preserves the integrity of the wood substrate.

Installation over Concrete Slabs

Installing over concrete requires specific management of moisture and thermal conductivity. Concrete acts as a massive heat sink; therefore, proper isolation is mandatory for efficiency.

  • Moisture Management: A 6-mil vapor barrier or moisture-rated underlayment must be laid first to protect the carbon composite elements and flooring from MVER.
  • Thermal Decoupling: To achieve 60% energy savings, the underlayment must decouple the heater from the cold thermal mass of the concrete, directing properties upward.
  • Leveling: The concrete slab must be level (typically 3/16" over 10 feet). Significant deviations require a high-strength self-leveler prior to moisture barrier installation.

Flooring Substrates

HexHeat provides a fast, clean installation pathway for various flooring types. The performance is intrinsically linked to the subfloor environment.

Installed on Plywood Substrate

Ideally suited for cementitious applications like stone and porcelain. For tile and stone, HexHeat is installed using a "bonded" pathway, providing maximum thermal transfer and durability.

  • The Bonded Foundation: A high-density, cementitious backer board or approved uncoupling membrane is secured to the plywood.
  • Mat Integration: The carbon composite mat is bonded to the underlayment using polymer-modified thin-set mortar. Total "stack height" remains significantly lower.
  • Thermal Distribution: Aerospace-grade carbon composite creates a uniform thermal plane, eliminating the "striping" effect common with resistive wires.

Installed on Concrete Slab

Concrete slabs provide excellent thermal mass but require specific preparation to manage moisture and ensure a permanent bond.

  • Surface Preparation: Slab must be clean, dry, and cured. Slabs with high MVER require a moisture mitigation system prior to mat installation.
  • Direct Bond Pathway: Embedded in thin-set directly on concrete (or over uncoupling membrane). The slab is utilized as a secondary thermal reservoir.
  • Crack Isolation: For slabs prone to movement, HexHeat can be integrated with industry-standard crack isolation membranes without compromising performance.
