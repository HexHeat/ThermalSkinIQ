Accelerating Design Forward

Based on our combined experience working with various building technologies and industries we have come to understand some primary rules with emerging technologies … change and evolution are the only constant in every business sector.

The world today has changed, our corporate culture has always maintained the position of strategic alignment with progressive manufacturing partners and technologies. With that innovative mindset in our corporate DNA, we understood from our customers early on there is real opportunity for innovators to capitalize on the product and design shortfalls in the commercial and residential building sectors.

From the beginning we recognized the importance of working alongside our trade and contractor partners directly to understand these product design limitations and shortfalls and to collaborate with our various innovative manufacturing partners to refine the current technologies. Introducing ThermalSkinIQ

Revolutionizing In-Floor Heating

How We Heat

The Innovation at the Core

At the heart of ThermalSkinIQ lies an advanced engineered prepreg carbon woven fabric composition—a highly efficient thermal conductor that delivers superior performance compared to traditional systems.

Imagine a home that warms you from the inside out – not by blasting hot air, but by gently awakening the very structure of your space.

ThermalSkinIQ represents a breakthrough in zoned primary radiant in-floor heating. At its core is an advanced engineered prepreg carbon woven fabric – a highly efficient thermal conductor that quietly redefines what efficient, safe, and deeply comfortable heating can be for homes and commercial spaces alike.

Why traditional heating falls short

Most electric convection heaters heat the air first – a slow, indirect process that wastes energy circulating warm air before it ever reaches you. In open rooms or poorly insulated buildings, this approach struggles, driving up costs and leaving uneven comfort.

What if the building itself became the heater?

ThermalSkinIQ works like a thermal superconductor. Our engineered prepreg carbon woven fabric composition transfers heat directly into the subfloor (concrete or wood), then radiates outward through far-infrared waves into walls, ceilings, and objects in the room. The result? Balanced, moderated temperatures with optimized energy use – no hot spots, no cold drafts.

This creates true homeostasis – stable, even comfort unique to radiant systems that heat the structure and envelope, not just the air.

Passive + Active synergy

  • Passive elements (insulation, vapor barriers, high-thermal-mass materials) absorb and store heat.
  • Active ThermalSkinIQ elements precisely balance temperature swings in every zone.

The outcome: superior comfort, dramatically lower energy demands, and running costs that surprise even skeptics.

Proven where it matters most

Developed over 10 years in collaboration with builders and developers, ThermalSkinIQ underwent rigorous, long-term field trials across North American climate zones starting in late 2021. These real-world case studies confirmed it meets – and often exceeds – the strict standards to become the first electric primary in-floor heating technology of its kind.

Revolutionary simplicity

By eliminating up to 90% of the mechanical components found in conventional primary heating systems, ThermalSkinIQ delivers cleaner installation, fewer failure points, and unmatched reliability.

Curious how this feels in real life?

Step onto a ThermalSkinIQ floor. Feel the gentle, all-over warmth that seems to come from everywhere and nowhere. No noisy fans. No visible vents. Just pure, efficient comfort – redefining what "home heating" can be.

The Next Evolution of Primary In-Floor Heating

From a homeowner, builder, or developer’s perspective, engineered prepreg carbon woven fabric heaters deliver a next-generation solution that checks all the boxes, energy efficiency, cost-effectiveness, ease of installation, and long-term reliability. These systems not only reduce operational costs for end users, but also provide consistent, uniform heat that enhances comfort in every space.

Their lightweight, low-profile design simplifies installation, reduces labor time, and minimizes callbacks thanks to their durable, low-maintenance construction. When paired with a renewable energy source, their near-zero environmental footprint positions you to meet growing demand for sustainable heating solutions, without compromising performance. Compared to gas or electric space heating systems, engineered prepreg carbon woven fabric heaters offer a cleaner, safer, and more future-proof option that contractors can install with confidence and clients will appreciate for years to come.
