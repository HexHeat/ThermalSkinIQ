ThermalSkinIQ transforms approved building surfaces into low-profile radiant-heating systems. Its InfraWeave technology uses a highly carbonized, orthogonally interlaced conductive fabric arranged in an engineered serpentine configuration to create broad-area radiant heat.

The difference is in the weave

Inside every ThermalSkinIQ surface is an advanced woven heating element

InfraWeave is a highly carbonized conductive fabric with an orthogonally interlaced architecture. The woven element is arranged in an engineered serpentine configuration across the panel assembly, creating a low-profile platform for broad-area radiant heating.

ThermalSkinIQ builds on this material foundation with scalable panel configurations, temperature sensing, independent zones and building-control integration.

Technical platform

Engineered for broad-area radiant heating

The ThermalSkinIQ platform is designed to distribute electrical resistance heating across the panel surface through an orthogonally interlaced carbonized fabric element. Heating-element geometry, pass spacing, thermal spreader, controls and panel output are coordinated to meet approved assembly and project requirements.

The platform supports residential and commercial applications, new construction and retrofit projects, and independent zone control across approved floor, wall and ceiling assemblies.
