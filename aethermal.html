The difference is in the weave

Material platform

Woven architecture. Engineered heating path.

InfraWeave™ is a highly carbonized conductive fabric with an orthogonally interlaced architecture. The element is arranged in an engineered serpentine configuration within the panel assembly to create a low-profile radiant-heating platform.

ThermalSkinIQ™ combines this material platform with configurable panel architecture and a roadmap for sensing, independent zones and building-control integration.

Technology summary
  • Carbon-fabric radiant heating element
  • Periodic, orthogonally interlaced woven geometry
  • Engineered serpentine configuration
  • Panel assembly designed for broad-area heating
  • Designed for integration into approved assemblies
