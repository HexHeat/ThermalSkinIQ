Advancing Carbon/Graphite Thermal Technologies

AEThermal Solutions Inc. is dedicated to advancing CFP-HY, Carbon/Graphite-based thermal technologies. With over two decades of expertise, we are revolutionizing power density draws and thermal characteristics by leveraging carbon/graphite's superior electrical, thermal, and mechanical properties to replace traditional metals like copper.

Our innovative approaches deliver exceptional performance gains, significant weight and cost savings, and unlock radical new applications to meet the ever-increasing electrical grid demand for energy-optimized, sustainable products.

State-of-the-Art In-House Laboratory

At the heart of our operations is a facility equipped with advanced tools allowing us to conduct precise diagnostics, R&D, and prototyping. This enables us to reinvent designs, like our collaboration to achieve the world's first UL 2683 certification for carbon composite primary heating.

Diagnostic Measurements

Equipped with specialized Keyence imaging digital microscopes for high-fidelity, real-time 2D and 3D measurements and material analysis.

Thermal Imaging Analysis

Advanced thermal IR imaging systems allow our engineers to map thermal flux, ensure uniform heat distribution, and eliminate structural stress points.

Tensile & Electrical Testing

Utilizing universal testing machines and advanced electrical testing systems to ensure absolute reliability, safety, and mechanical integrity of our carbon/graphite materials.

A World-Class Engineering Team

Backed by a team of engineers and scientists with decades of experience in clean energy sectors—including hydrogen fuel cells, aerospace, and renewables—AEThermal Solutions is positioned to become one of North America's leading graphite labs.

Our Mission: To develop market-driven products with a focus on thermal heating systems and beyond.

Our Vision: Extending our reach to integrate graphene into transformative technologies such as solar panels, heat storage, fabric infusions, and advanced building materials.
