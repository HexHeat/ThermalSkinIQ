Project planning & design

Bring architectural, electrical and thermal requirements together early to evaluate a ThermalSkinIQ application.

Planning framework

Coordinate the whole assembly

Use this overview to organize a project discussion. It is not a substitute for approved drawings, installation documentation or qualified professional design.

01

Define the application

Identify the intended floor, wall or ceiling location, project type, room use and construction stage.

02

Document the assembly

Record the substrate, proposed finished surface, underlayment and other relevant layers for review.

03

Coordinate design

Have qualified project professionals coordinate heat-loss needs, panel layout, circuits, controls and applicable requirements.

04

Confirm product fit

Verify compatibility, certifications, installation method and performance limits using current approved system documents.

Technical review before specification

Electrical configuration, resistance, wattage, current, terminals, controls, sensors, safety devices and finished-surface compatibility must be established for the specific product and assembly. Do not rely on concept copy as engineering guidance.

Project information to gather

  • Project location and building type
  • Plans or room dimensions
  • Proposed surface and assembly details
  • Electrical design constraints identified by the project team
  • Desired zones, control approach and project schedule
Design note

ThermalSkinIQ is intended for integration into approved assemblies. Final product availability, voltage configurations, certification status and installation details must be confirmed before specification or construction.
